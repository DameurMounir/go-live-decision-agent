# Research foundations

The public decision model is informed by the following primary-source frameworks
and operational guidance. It does **not** claim certification, conformance, or
coverage equivalence against any of them.

- [NIST Cybersecurity Framework 2.0](https://www.nist.gov/publications/nist-cybersecurity-framework-csf-20)
  contributes the outcome-oriented risk-management view and the Govern,
  Identify, Protect, Detect, Respond, and Recover lifecycle.
- [NIST SP 800-218, Secure Software Development Framework 1.1](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/218/final)
  contributes secure-software preparation, production, vulnerability response,
  and evidence-oriented supplier communication practices.
- [AWS Operational Readiness Reviews](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/operational-readiness-reviews/wa-operational-readiness-reviews.html)
  contributes repeatable readiness questions, action tracking, lifecycle review,
  and the use of operational lessons to prevent known causes of impact.
- [AWS Well-Architected operational readiness guidance](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/operational-excellence-pillar/operational-readiness.html)
  contributes consistent go-live checklists, runbooks, playbooks, support plans,
  and explicit preparation for unsuccessful changes and rollback.
- [OWASP Application Security Verification Standard 5.0](https://owasp.org/www-project-application-security-verification-standard/)
  contributes explicit, versioned application-security verification
  requirements and a measurable basis for security assurance.

The repository translates those ideas into a synthetic, evidence-bound decision
contract. It does not reproduce proprietary checklists, replace organizational
policy, or grant release authority.
